The kingdom

A storybook map of 14 regions

Walk from Pawn Village to the Library of Classic Games. Short interactive lessons, a placement test that starts you at the right spot, gate challenges and a letter in your journal every day. Lessons unlock in order: finish one to open the next.

Pawn Village

Where every journey begins: cottages shaped like pawns, soft checkerboard fields and little bridges.

City of the Pieces

A lively town of rook towers, bishop spires and a queen’s palace on the hill.

Fortress of Rules

Banners, a drawbridge and a king’s keep above the mist.

Tactics Forest

Lantern-lit paths, stone pieces as statues and forks in the road.

Friendly residents

Every region has its own people to meet. They cheer you on, set you challenges and, after a game, will talk it through with you.

Meet Firefly

A lantern, a knight, a friend

Firefly, a lantern-carrying knight, greets you each day, cheers your good moves and shows the way when you are stuck. A translucent piece glides along the move to make, so you see the idea instead of just reading it.

Three gentle levels of hints

Ask for a little help or a lot. The lantern only burns as bright as you need.

  1. 1 A first nudgeA gentle hint about which idea to look for.
  2. 2 A closer lookPoints to the piece or the square that matters.
  3. 3 The move itselfShows the best move, so you can see why it works.

Train

Small steps, every day

  • Bite-size lessons

    Short, interactive lessons you can finish in a few minutes, with a three-level lantern hint whenever you get stuck.

  • Puzzles for your level

    Tactics, checkmate patterns, openings and endgames, picked to match how you play.

The coach

A coach on your device, always by your side

Ask the coach while you play, get hints, and review every game afterwards. After a game, Ask the Resident for a short chat about your mistakes, your best move or what to try next.

Explanations are generated on your device, with Apple Intelligence when available and built-in templates otherwise. The engine always makes the evaluation.

Private by design

Everything stays on your device

The engine, the coach, your games and your stats all run on your device. Your games, photos and coach chats never leave it.

  • No accountsOpen the app and play.
  • No adsNever, anywhere in the app.
  • No trackingNo data collection of any kind.
  • Works offlineOn a plane or off the grid.

    Vietnamese, English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), German and French. Lessons are in Vietnamese and English.
